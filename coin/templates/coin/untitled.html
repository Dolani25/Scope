Ordinary Differential Equation (ODE)

An Ordinary Differential Equation is a mathematical equation that relates a function to its derivatives with respect to one or more independent variables. It typically takes the form:

dy/dx = f(x, y)

Where:

  • dy/dx represents the derivative of the function y with respect to the independent variable x.
  • f(x, y) is a function that defines how the rate of change of y depends on the values of x and y.

ODEs are fundamental in modeling various physical, biological, and engineering phenomena, and solving them helps us understand how systems evolve over time.
